## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Hydra - Water Tracker & Health Manager** ("Hydra", "we", "our", or "us").

Your privacy is our priority. Hydra is designed with an **offline-first** approach, meaning your personal health information remains on your device whenever possible.

We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal information.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### Health & Activity Data (Stored Locally)

Hydra may store the following information **only on your Android device**:

- Daily water intake logs
- Hydration goals
- Sleep records
- Step counts
- Body weight
- Height
- Age
- Gender
- Personal preferences
- Reminder settings

### Purpose

This information is used to:

- Calculate personalized hydration goals
- Display statistics and progress
- Track hydration history
- Adjust recommendations based on your profile
- Provide reminders and analytics

### Storage

Your health data is stored locally inside the app's private database on your device.

Unless you explicitly export or back up your data, it is **not transmitted to our servers**.

---

## 3. Permissions

Hydra may request the following Android permissions.

### Physical Activity (`ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`)

Used to:

- Read daily step counts
- Estimate activity level
- Improve hydration recommendations

This information remains on your device.

---

### Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)

Used to:

- Send hydration reminders
- Send goal notifications
- Display scheduled reminders

Notifications are generated locally by your device.

---

### Storage Access

Used only when you choose to:

- Export backups
- Import backups
- Restore data

Hydra never accesses your files without your action.

---

## 4. Weather Service

Hydra can optionally retrieve weather information to improve hydration recommendations.

When using this feature:

- Your selected city or weather query may be sent to a weather service.
- No health information is included.
- No payment information is included.
- No personal profile information is shared.

---

## 5. Premium Subscription

Hydra offers optional Premium subscriptions through **Google Play Billing**.

Google securely processes:

- Payments
- Renewals
- Subscription management

We do **not** receive or store:

- Credit card numbers
- Bank account details
- Billing addresses
- Payment credentials

Subscription validation is handled through Google Play.

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal information.

We do **not** share your health data with advertisers.

We do **not** upload your hydration history to our servers.

---

## 7. Data Security

Your information is protected using Android's application sandbox.

Only the Hydra application can access its private database.

We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock or biometric authentication.

---

## 8. Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until:

- You delete it
- You clear app storage
- You uninstall the app

If you export a backup, that exported file is managed entirely by you.

---

## 9. Your Rights

You may, at any time:

- View your data
- Modify your data
- Export your data
- Import backups
- Delete all data
- Uninstall the application

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data unless you created a backup.

---

## 10. Children's Privacy

Hydra is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because Hydra does not require account registration and stores data locally, we do not maintain user databases containing children's personal information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be reflected by changing the **Effective Date** at the top of this document.

The latest version will also be available within the app.
